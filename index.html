    How this is calculated
    • Patients a year = new patients a month × 12.
    • Patients who stop early attend about half of their plan, on average.
    • Left on the table = patients who stop × half the plan’s visits × revenue per visit.
    • Visits kept = the extra patients who finish × half the plan’s visits × revenue per visit.
    • RTM = enrolled patients × months in care (visits ÷ 2 a week) × monthly RTM rate.
    • Illustrative only. These are not Revora’s published figures.

    Meet Clara, your companion who keeps the plan moving

    Soreness or setback, a question about form, a nudge on a hard day. Clara meets patients where they are, in words they understand.
    Some things need clinical judgment. Clara flags those and passes them to the clinic, so nothing important waits for the next visit.
    Clinicians make every clinical decision. Clara does not diagnose, prescribe, or change a program.
